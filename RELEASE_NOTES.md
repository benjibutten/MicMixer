# What's changed

<!-- Update this list together with user-visible changes under src/. -->

- **The music volume sliders follow your ears.** They used to change the level
  in a straight line, so the music was still clearly audible at 1 % and the
  whole quiet range sat in the bottom few percent. Both sliders, Music volume
  and Monitoring, now change evenly in loudness.
  Your saved levels sound the same as before; the sliders just sit higher up.
  StreamDecky shows the same percentage as MicMixer.
- **Monitoring on a surround output plays from the front speakers only.** On an
  output set up as 5.1 or 7.1, such as a headset with virtual surround, the right
  channel of the music was also copied to the centre, bass and rear channels,
  which made it far louder than the slider said. Voice profile previews were
  copied to every channel the same way.
